Voidborne Tactics

A turn-based space tactics game for Android where every battle is a puzzle of positioning, timing, and nerve. Build your fleet ship by ship, lead it with admirals who grow alongside you, and outwit enemies who fight nothing like each other — from pirate swarms to dying empires guarding impossible technology.

Tactical combat with real decisions

No auto-win lineups, no damage-sponge grind. Choose your engagement range, set your formation, issue repair orders, and play command cards at the moment they matter. A clever draw should feel like a lightning strike — and a bad one should still leave you a way to fight through.

Deep fleet building

Dozens of hulls from nimble corvettes to dreadnoughts, each with weapon slots, armor, shields, and specialized systems. Mount oversized guns on small frames, field carriers with hangars full of strike craft, and level every part of your fleet. Your designs are yours — every loadout is a thesis about how a battle should go.

A story worth fighting through

A multi-chapter campaign follows the Federation into a war that keeps getting stranger. Real characters, real losses, and an enemy whose story is bigger than it first appears. New chapters ship regularly — and the plot is the point, not a coat of paint.

Fight fleets built by real players

Quick Battle matches you against fleet designs uploaded by other commanders — real loadouts, real tactics, rated fairly by fleet strength, so victories are earned, not bought. Between matches: weekly bosses with unique mechanics, daily missions, a deep achievement board, and local PVP to settle arguments with friends directly.

Five factions, five doctrines

Disciplined Federation lines, Saurian boarding packs who answer superior technology with knives, opportunistic pirates, the haunted remnants of the Ancients — and something older, still counting.
